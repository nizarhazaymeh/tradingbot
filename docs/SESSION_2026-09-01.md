## Bugs found by running live — 8, all fixed

Every one of these was invisible in dry-run:

1. **Stale-quote pricing.** Orders were priced from the chain fetched at the start
   of the cycle. The first live order asked \$0.25 credit when, seconds later, the
   market offered \$0.14. It never filled. Now quotes are refreshed immediately
   before pricing, and a fill-chasing ladder replaces the order at progressively
   worse prices until it fills.
2. **Orphan-creating NameError.** A stale variable reference raised *after* the
   order reached the broker but *before* it was recorded — leaving a live 4-leg
   condor with no exit plan. Since Alpaca has no bracket orders on options, that
   monitor loop is the only stop-loss.
3. **Positions recorded on submit, not on fill.** An unfilled condor was tracked
   as open, so the monitor tried to manage legs the broker did not hold.
4. **Ladder sign bug.** The final rung moved a credit further negative, asking for
   *more* credit and making a fill *less* likely.
5. **Critic vetoing valid trades.** The regime summary prescribed a structure, so
   when the EV optimiser chose a different one the LLM critic called it a
   contradiction. Regime strings are now descriptive.
6. **🔴 EV ignored transaction costs.** The biggest one. Every position the agent
   opened had a round-trip bid/ask *larger than the EV the gate required* — a
   condor needing \$8.82 of EV paid \$36 in spread. It was losing money by
   design. EV is now net of round-trip cost.
7. **Duplicate bets.** Two SPY condors opened with identical short strikes
   [755, 773]. One bet twice, paying the spread twice.
8. **🔴 Shared ledger across accounts.** The first COMP cycle read DEV's seven
   positions, so COMP's risk budget was consumed by another account's heat and
   every trade was rejected. The account would have sat flat with no error
   visible. The ledger is now per-account.

Bugs 2, 6 and 8 would each have been quietly expensive. 6 and 8 were found only
because I inspected single cycles instead of starting a loop.
